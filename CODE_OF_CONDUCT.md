# Iteron Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Iteron is built by people with different backgrounds, identities, locations,
and levels of experience. We are committed to a community where participation is
safe, respectful, and focused on the work. This applies equally to maintainers,
contributors, users, and people representing the project in public spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas, code, evidence, and outcomes rather than people.
- Make room for less experienced contributors and explain project-specific
  context without condescension.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and stated boundaries.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and correct mistakes openly.
- Accept a maintainer's request to pause, narrow, or move a discussion.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, doxxing, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Discriminatory language or conduct, including coded or repeated hostility.
- Personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.
- Using an agent, bot, alternate account, or private channel to evade moderation.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional public community.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, Discussions, release
channels, project events, and private project communication. It also applies when
someone is publicly representing Iteron or using a project role to influence a
related interaction.

Technical disagreement is expected. Repetition, status, or claimed intent does
not excuse harmful conduct.

## Reporting

For a confidential report, use the repository's private
[Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/Plantcore-AI/Iteron/security/advisories/new)
form and prefix the title with `[Conduct]`. The human Project Owner receives this
private intake. Do not include a conduct report in a public issue.

Include only what is needed to understand the incident: where it occurred, the
people involved, relevant links or screenshots, and any immediate safety concern.
Reports are shared only with people required to assess and resolve them.

During bootstrap, the Project Owner is the sole internal enforcement contact. If
a report concerns the Owner, use
[GitHub's abuse-reporting channel](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse)
instead; the project does not pretend to offer independent internal adjudication
before independent maintainers are appointed.

## Enforcement

The Project Owner may take one or more proportionate actions:

1. private correction and a request to change behavior;
2. a formal warning with explicit conditions;
3. temporary limits on project interaction;
4. removal of project privileges or a permanent community ban.

Serious threats, harassment, disclosure of private information, or retaliation
may move directly to a stronger response. Enforcement decisions should record the
reason and scope while protecting reporter privacy. Good-faith reports will not be
punished merely because the evidence is inconclusive.
